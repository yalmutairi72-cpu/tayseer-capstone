# Q&A Preparation

Answer, acknowledge uncertainty if there is any, then return to the big idea: *four regions won't close the gap on their own, so the SAR 40M goes there.*

**"The national number is already above 65%. Why spend anything?"**
The target was national, but the 8 regions below it serve real citizens. Najran would need about 21 months at its current pace. The money buys equal access, not a better average.

**"Why not fund all 8 below-target regions?"**
Asir, Tabuk, Hail and Al-Jouf are within 0.7 pts and will cross 65% in about 1 to 3 months at their 2025 pace. Funding them puts half the budget where the gap closes anyway (slide 4).

**"How do you know SAR 40M is enough?"**
We don't. The data shows where the gap is, not how much a point of adoption costs. That's why only SAR 20M is released now, with a June 2027 checkpoint at halfway to 65%. If a region misses it, its second tranche is re-targeted.

**"Why does Najran get SAR 15M?"**
The split is proportional to users still to convert (gap × users). Najran has the biggest gap (4.1 pts) *and* the slowest pace (+0.19 pts a month), so it needs to roughly double its pace.

**"What will the money actually be spent on?"**
Complaints is the weakest journey in these regions (56.8% digital vs 62.1% nationally). After that, Permits and Business Services. Branches have the lowest adoption (53%) of any channel, so assisted-digital desks there convert walk-in users.

**"Is there a financial return?"**
It's secondary, but positive: a digital transaction costs about SAR 15, against about SAR 24 for branch or call centre, and digital CSAT is higher (4.1 vs 3.8). We don't claim the 40M pays for itself.

**"How did you calculate pace / months to target?"**
Linear trend of each region's monthly user-weighted adoption over 2025, then gap ÷ pace. The Tableau year-over-year version gives the same four regions.

**"Is the trend reliable?"**
National adoption has risen steadily for 4 years with no reversals, so a linear trend is reasonable for a 6 to 12 month view. Beyond that, treat it as directional. The checkpoint protects against it being wrong.

**Data caveat:** the dataset is synthetic training data. Treat the findings as the method you would apply to real Tayseer data.
