## Yousef Almutairi (0:00 – 1:15)

### Slide 1: BLUF / The Ask (0:00 – 0:30)

Good morning. I'm Yousef, with Abdulwahab and Firas.

**We are asking you to approve SAR 40 million for four regions: Najran, Northern Borders, Al-Baha and Jazan.**

Tayseer has already passed the 65% digital-adoption target nationally. But these four regions are stuck below it, and on their current pace they won't get there soon. Najran alone would need almost two years.

Our recommendation puts every riyal where the gap won't close by itself, released in two tranches with a checkpoint in June 2026.

*[Next slide]*

### Slide 2: Situation (0:30 – 1:15)

Let's start with where we stand nationally.

*[Point at the line]* This line is national digital adoption every month from January 2022 to December 2025. The dashed orange line is the 65% target.

Adoption grew steadily from about 54% to **66.2%** in December 2025. **We crossed the 65% target in August 2025.**

So if we only looked at the national number, we'd say the job is done.

But the national average hides something important, and Abdulwahab will show you what.

*[Hand over to Abdulwahab]*

---

## Abdulwahab Alnassar (1:15 – 4:00)

### Slide 3: Complication (1:15 – 2:00)

Thank you, Yousef.

*[Point at the chart]* Each bar here is one region's digital adoption in December 2025, sorted from lowest to highest. Orange means below the 65% target; grey means on or above it. The dashed line is 65%.

**Eight of our thirteen regions are still below the target.**

The national average is above 65% because five large regions, Riyadh, Makkah, the Eastern Province, Madinah and Qassim, pull it up. Riyadh and Makkah are above 70%.

At the bottom is **Najran at 60.9%, more than four points short.**

But not every orange bar is equally urgent, and that matters a lot for where the money should go.

*[Next slide]*

### Slide 4: Evidence (2:00 – 4:00)

This is the key slide of our analysis.

*[Point at the chart]* We took only the eight regions below target and asked one question: **if each region keeps its 2025 pace, how many months until it reaches 65%?** That's the length of each bar. The labels show the gap in points and how fast each region is improving per month.

*[Point at the grey bars]* The top four in grey, Al-Jouf, Hail, Tabuk and Asir, are less than one point away. At their current pace they'll cross 65% in **one to three months, on their own.** They don't need this money.

*[Point at the orange bars]* The bottom four are a different story. Jazan needs about 7 months, Al-Baha 9, Northern Borders 10, and **Najran needs 21 months.** Najran has the biggest gap and the slowest pace, only 0.19 points a month. Time alone won't fix it.

*[Point at the box on the right]* We also looked at *where* the gap sits inside these four regions. The weakest service is **Complaints: only 56.8% digital, against 62.1% nationally.** And every service category in these regions trails the national rate by three to five points, so this is a regional problem, not a problem with one product.

So the evidence points to four regions, and Firas will now walk you through how to spend the SAR 40 million.

*[Hand over to Firas]*

---

## Firas Alnasser (4:00 – 7:00)

### Slide 5: Options (4:00 – 5:00)

Thank you, Abdulwahab.

We compared three realistic ways to use the SAR 40 million, and judged each one by the same question: **does the money reach a gap that won't close by itself?**

**Option A: spread it evenly**, SAR 5 million to each of the eight regions below target. It's simple and feels fair. But half the money, SAR 20 million, goes to regions that will cross 65% within three months anyway, and Najran gets only 5 million.

**Option B: focus on the four lagging regions**, with the money split by the size of each gap. 100% of the budget goes to regions that need 7 to 21 months.

**Option C: one national campaign** to move users to the app and the website. But we're already at 66% nationally, and most of its reach lands in regions that are already above target.

*[Next slide]*

### Slide 6: Recommendation (5:00 – 6:15)

**We recommend Option B.**

*[Point at the chart]* The split follows the number of users who still need to switch to digital: **Najran 15 million, Northern Borders 9, Al-Baha 8 and Jazan 8.**

What does the money buy? Three things. First, digitising the weakest journeys, Complaints and Permits. Second, assisted-digital desks inside branches, because branches have the lowest adoption of any channel: 53%, compared with 75% for the mobile app. Third, local awareness and onboarding in the four regions.

The expected effect: **all four regions at 65% or above by December 2026.** For Najran, that means roughly doubling its pace, from 0.19 to 0.34 points a month. Without this investment, Najran reaches the target around September 2027.

To be honest about uncertainty: our data shows *where* the gap is, but not exactly how many points each million buys. That's why we propose releasing the money in stages.

*[Next slide]*

### Slide 7: The Ask & Next Step (6:15 – 7:00)

So our ask is clear.

**Approve SAR 40 million for Najran, Northern Borders, Al-Baha and Jazan: SAR 20 million now, and SAR 20 million in June 2026.**

The second tranche is released region by region, only if that region is at least halfway to 65%. Those checkpoint values are on the slide, from Najran at 62.9% to Jazan at 64.0%, and we track them monthly on our Tableau dashboard. If a region misses its checkpoint, its second tranche goes to its weakest service or to another region that is falling behind.

**Nationally, Tayseer has hit 65%. This decision makes sure every region does.**

Thank you. We're happy to take your questions.

Ready answers are in [`qa_prep.md`](qa_prep.md). Always finish an answer by coming back to the main point: **four regions won't close the gap on their own, so the money goes there.**

## Rehearsal tips
- Rehearse together with a stopwatch at least twice. The total must not go over 7 minutes.
- The Ask has to be said within the first 30 seconds (Yousef) and again at the close (Firas).
- On every chart, first say what the chart shows, then the conclusion.
- Hand over by name: "...and Abdulwahab will show you...".
